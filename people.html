Founder & CEO

Jay Prakash Pai

B.Com, CFPCM, ACS and LLB, with about 25 years in financial services. Before founding WMU he headed HSBC Private Banking for Southern India for about 5 years, personally managing the bank’s top 20 private banking relationships in the region. Earlier: Deutsche Bank Private Wealth Management (about 7 years) and Canfin Homes, Canara Bank’s housing loan subsidiary (5 years). Private Banking Certification, Wealth Management Institute of Singapore.

Interests

Ironman (Copenhagen, August 2019) and ultra marathoner. Trekking, travelling and golf.

Co Founder & COO

Kavitha Jayaram

Heads operations, making sure clients get high-quality support every day. She began at Xerox Engineering in 1995, then spent 13 years at HSBC from 1997 — across retail banking operations, business development, trade services, internal control, compliance and audits. For over 3 years she served ultra high net-worth clients as Associate Vice President – Client Services, HSBC Private Banking.

Interests

Avid runner — several full and half marathons — and keen traveller.

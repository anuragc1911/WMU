Equities

One of the most vibrant asset classes, with many ways to participate.

For active funds we choose managers who consistently outperform. For broad or tactical exposure, we buy indices directly (ETFs or futures).

02 / Fixed Income

Fixed Income

Part of every portfolio — it brings stability and makes rebalancing effective.

We buy government bonds directly, and invest in investment-grade and high-yield bonds through active funds and indices. Credit rating, duration and currency exposure are actively managed by WMU’s investment team.

Offerings

  • 01/Debt Mutual Funds
  • 02/Government and Corporate Bonds
  • 03/Bank and Corporate deposits
  • 04/Structured products
  • 05/Foreign currency Bonds
